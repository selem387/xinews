Project 1 · Xinhua Media Buying

Mexican voices for Chinese brands

Xinhua hires content creators with 10,000 to 300,000 followers to talk about Chinese brands in Mexico, and charges those brands monthly packages, campaigns and licenses. Content is labeled as advertising and never mixed with the news.

What

Reels and videos from growing creators; 3 monthly packages, campaigns, licenses and ambassadors

Who pays

60 to 80 Chinese consumer brands: cars, phones, home, e-commerce, food and apps

Split

Creator ~60%; margin ~40% in three equal parts: Xinhua, Intiviso and whoever sells

Project 2 · MX News

News and advertising on the −1 screen

A portal of Xinhua news and Chinese company advertising, preinstalled on the −1 screen of Chinese-brand phones (56% of the phones sold in Mexico). Phone brands get 70% of ad revenue; Chinese advertisers pay less than with Taboola and Google.

What

A −1 screen with Xinhua and local news, and Chinese brand advertising at a preferential price

Who pays

Chinese advertisers; phone brands provide the space and receive 70%

Split

Of the remaining 30%: 10% Xinhua, 10% Intiviso and 10% whoever sells

Project 3 · China–Latam Business Growth

From market entry to sales

Market intelligence, prospecting of buyers and distributors, qualified meetings and sales automation for Chinese companies that sell or will sell in Mexico. Charged as implementation, a monthly fee, and a charge per qualified meeting and per accepted opportunity.

What

Four services measured with transparent indicators; 3 packages: Entry, Growth and Expansion

Who pays

300 to 500 Chinese companies selling in Mexico, plus 30 to 42 new ones a year
