Xinhua news and advertising from Chinese companies, one swipe away on Chinese-brand mobile phones.

Objective

For Xinhua to contribute to the success of Chinese companies by creating a preferential space for advertising and mass visibility on Chinese-brand mobile phones.

The proposal

Purpose

Xinhua does not seek its own benefit: it seeks the benefit of all of China, through the global success of Chinese companies, starting with Latin America.

The proposal

Strategy

Create a preferential space for Chinese brands on Chinese phones: the phone brands provide the advertising space and other Chinese brands provide the advertising.

The proposal

The space

The −1 screen of Chinese-brand phones, one swipe away, with Xinhua and local news, and highly preferential advertising rates for Chinese companies.

56%of phones sold in Mexico are Chinese brands (Omdia, Q2 2026)

The proposal

How we win

Xinhua will offer Chinese phone brands a higher share of advertising revenue than Taboola and Google give them today, and Chinese advertisers a lower cost per advert than Taboola and Google charge them today.

We also reserve a free space for the brand to sell phone renewals and upgrades to its existing customers.

The proposal

Why we win

With phone brands

Today, Chinese brands that do not work with Taboola receive no additional advertising revenue. Where they do, Taboola pays out 63% of revenue on average. Xinhua can improve on that percentage.
