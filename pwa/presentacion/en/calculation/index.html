Digital spend

$1,900 to $3,100 million MXN per year

What Chinese brands invest in digital advertising in Mexico.

How it is reached

  1. Total advertising spend of Chinese brands is estimated across three sectors (table below): $2,900 to $4,750 million MXN.
  2. The Mexican market's digital share is applied: 66.6% of total advertising investment in 2025 ($124,625 of $187,155 million MXN, Valor Total Media 2026).
  3. $2,900 × 0.66 = $1,914 million MXN and $4,750 × 0.66 = $3,135 million MXN; rounded: $1,900 to $3,100 million MXN.

Display spend

$260 to $430 million MXN per year

The part of digital spend that goes to display, the media and portal format where MX News competes.

How it is reached

  1. Display was 13.8% of Mexico's digital investment in 2025 ($17,233 of $124,625 million MXN, Valor Total Media 2026). The rest is video (62.5%) and search (22.4%).
  2. $1,900 × 0.138 = $262 million MXN and $3,100 × 0.138 = $428 million MXN; rounded: $260 to $430 million MXN.

Assumption to validate: Chinese brands are assumed to split their digital spend the same way the market does. If they invest more in video and social than the average, display would be lower.

Pilot scenario

$40 million MXN per year

Year 1, with 1 or 2 phone brands.

How it is reached

  1. By spend: 2% of Chinese brands' digital spend is captured. $1,900 × 0.02 = $38 million MXN, rounded to $40 million MXN.
  2. By audience, as a cross-check: 1.3 million active users × US$1.6 per user per year = $2.1 million USD × 19.20 = $40 million MXN. Advertising yield per user ranges from US$1 to US$3 per year ($25 to $75 million MXN), calculated from UNOTV data: 683 million impressions per month, eCPM of US$1 to US$3 and 7.4 million unique users.
  3. The 1.3 million users are a working assumption: 1 or 2 brands with about 14% of the roughly 32 million phones sold per year would be 4.5 million new devices; with 30% active users, that gives 1.3 million MXN.

Assumption to validate: the number of new devices and the activation rate depend on the brand and on the type of preinstallation negotiated.

Base scenario

$100 million MXN per year

Alliance with 3 or 4 phone brands.

How it is reached

  1. 4% of Chinese brands' digital spend is captured at its midpoint. The midpoint of $1,900 to $3,100 million MXN is $2,500 million MXN.
  2. $2,500 × 0.04 = $100 million MXN.
  3. This equals about 29% of Chinese brands' display spend ($100 of $345 million MXN, midpoint). It is a high share and assumes MX News captures a good part of Chinese brands' display; it is detailed in the reasonableness test.
High scenario

$190 million MXN per year

All phone brands, by year three.

How it is reached

  1. 6% of Chinese brands' digital spend is captured at its high end of $3,100 million MXN.
  2. $3,100 × 0.06 = $186 million MXN, rounded to $190 million MXN.
  3. This equals between 44% and 73% of Chinese brands' display ($190 of $430 or of $260 million MXN). It is a ceiling, not a target.

Reasonableness test

What share of Chinese brands' display each scenario represents

ScenarioRevenueAgainst display of $260 to $430 million MXN
Pilot$40 million MXN9% to 15%
Base$100 million MXN23% to 38%
High$190 million MXN44% to 73%

The pilot is defensible. The base already requires capturing about a quarter or more of Chinese brands' display, and the high scenario requires more than half at the low end. That is why the base scenario depends on MX News persuading advertisers to move budget from video and social, and on the medium being exclusive to Chinese brands.
